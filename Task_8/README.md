# 📊 Task 8: Simple Sales Dashboard Design

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this task is to create a simple and interactive sales dashboard using **Power BI** to analyze sales performance across categories, regions, months, and quarters. The dashboard is designed to present key business information in a clear and user-friendly format.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- 📊 **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- 📁 **CSV** – Data source
- 📈 **Power BI Visuals** – Charts, KPIs, slicers, and map visualization

## 📌 Dashboard Features

The dashboard provides an overview of sales performance through:

- 💰 **Total Sales** – $2.26M
- 💵 **Total Profit** – $0.41M
- 🛒 **Total Orders** – 4,922
- 📦 **Total Quantity** – 54K
- 🍩 **Sales by Category** – Donut chart showing sales contribution by category
- 📊 **Sales by Region** – Bar chart comparing regional sales performance
- 🗺️ **Sales by State** – Geographic visualization of sales distribution
- 📈 **Sales over Months** – Line chart showing monthly sales trends
- 📅 **Sales over Quarters** – Line chart showing quarterly sales performance
- 🔘 **Interactive Filters** – Region, Category, and Year slicers

## 🔍 Key Insights

1. 🥇 **Technology is the top-performing category**, generating approximately **$827K (36.6%)** of total sales.

2. 🌎 **West is the highest-performing region**, with approximately **$0.71M** in sales, followed by East ($0.67M), Central ($0.49M), and South ($0.39M).

3. 📈 **Sales increased strongly toward the end of the year**, reaching the highest monthly sales of approximately **$0.35M in November**, followed by $0.32M in December.

4. 🚀 **Q4 is the strongest quarter**, reaching approximately **$0.87M** in sales compared with **$0.35M in Q1**, showing strong sales momentum toward the end of the year.
