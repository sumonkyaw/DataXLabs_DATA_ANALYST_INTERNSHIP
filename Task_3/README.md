# Superstore Sales & Forecasting Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard providing an end-to-end analysis of Superstore sales performance, customer segmentation, regional metrics, and predictive sales forecasting.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

* **Total Sales:** $2.26M
* **Total Profit:** $0.41M
* **Total Orders:** 4,922
* **Total Quantity Sold:** 54K

---

## 📊 Dashboard Views & Features

### 1. Sales Overview Page
Provides executive-level KPIs and multi-dimensional analysis of sales distribution.

* **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Instant summary of total revenue, profit margin, volume, and order totals.
* **Sales by Segment:** Donut chart breakdown showing **Consumer** (50.76% / $1.15M), **Corporate** (30.44% / $0.69M), and **Home Office** (18.79% / $0.42M).
* **Sales & Profit by Category:** Stacked visual comparing revenue against profitability for *Technology*, *Furniture*, and *Office Supplies*.
* **Ship Mode Preference:** Bar visual showing order volume across *Standard Class*, *Second Class*, *First Class*, and *Same Day*.
* **Hierarchical Decomposition Tree:** Drill-down paths analyzing sales granularly: `State` ➔ `City` ➔ `Sub-Category` ➔ `Product Name`.

### 2. Sales & Forecasting Page
Delivers deep historical trend evaluation paired with predictive modeling for future strategic planning.

* **Quarterly & Monthly Seasonality:**
  * **Quarterly Trend:** Revenue growth from **Q1 ($0.35M)** to a peak in **Q4 ($0.87M)**.
  * **Monthly Trend:** Identifies peak purchasing months (November hitting **$0.35M**).
* **Yearly Performance:** Highlights sustained growth from **2015 ($0.48M)** to **2018 ($0.72M)**.
* **Next Year Sales Forecasting:** Time-series projection visual forecasting **2019 sales** complete with shaded confidence interval boundaries.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters & Slicers

The dashboard includes dynamic filtering across all views:
* **By Category:** `Furniture` | `Office Supplies` | `Technology`
* **By Region:** `Central` | `East` | `South` | `West`
* **By Segment:** `Consumer` | `Corporate` | `Home Office`

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. **Strong Seasonality:** Sales consistently rise towards the end of the calendar year, reaching peak levels in Q4 and November.
2. **Core Customer Base:** The **Consumer** segment drives over half of all total revenue ($1.15M).
3. **Logistics Preference:** **Standard Class** shipping dominates order distribution compared to expedited shipping options.
